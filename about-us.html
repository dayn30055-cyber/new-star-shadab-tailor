ABOUT OUR SHOP

About New Star Shadab Tailor

New Star Shadab Tailor is a local tailoring shop serving customers in Sujanganj, Jaunpur. We focus on accurate measurement, neat stitching, comfortable fitting and dependable customer service.

What we stitch

We provide custom stitching for shirts, pants, suits, blazers, kurtas, sherwanis and school uniforms, along with professional clothing alterations.

Our approach

Every order starts with understanding the customer’s preferred fit and style. Measurements, garment details and finishing requirements are checked carefully before stitching.
