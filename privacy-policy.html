Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

New Star Shadab Tailor respects your privacy. This page explains how information may be handled when you use this website.

Information you provide

If you use appointment, measurement or design-reference features, you may enter information such as your name, mobile number, preferred service, measurements, notes or a design image.

How information is used

Information you choose to send is used only to respond to tailoring enquiries, appointment requests, measurements, design references and order-related communication.

Local browser storage

Some features may store appointment or measurement information locally in your browser so the website can remember it on the same device. This information is not automatically sent to the shop unless you choose to share it, for example through WhatsApp.

Third-party services

The website may open third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps and Google Calendar. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact New Star Shadab Tailor through the details on the Contact Us page.
