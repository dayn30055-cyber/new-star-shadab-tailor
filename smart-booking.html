Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking form above and tap the WhatsApp button. Your details will be prepared in a message for the shop.

Is the online estimate the final price?

No. It is an indicative range. The final amount depends on the garment, design, fabric and fitting requirements and is confirmed by the tailor.

Yes, alteration services are available along with custom stitching.
